How to survive being pulled away from Lake Erie shore

Stay calm, float to conserve energy, and never try to swim directly back toward shore; instead, let the current carry you until it weakens, then swim parallel to the beach to slip out of the fast-moving channel,and once free, angle back in with the waves while signaling for help with raised arms.

How to survive falling in Lake Erie

Stay calm, keep your head above water, and immediately focus on floating or treading while orienting yourself away from waves or structures; avoid panicking or thrashing, scan for the nearest stable exit point like a ladder, pier edge, or calmer water zone, and if waves or currents are pushing you, let them carry you until you can safely angle toward shore, all while signaling for help with one arm raised so rescuers or bystanders can spot you quickly.
